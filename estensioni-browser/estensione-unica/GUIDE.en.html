Version 1.6.3

Sottotitoli Doppi + Parole al Volo — browser extension

Versione italiana → LEGGIMI

Learn languages by watching videos and reading. One extension for two things:

  • 🎬 in videos (Sottotitoli Doppi): under the original subtitles you see the translation in your language, in a different colour. Every word can be hovered to see its meaning in that sentence, hear it and save it to your personal vocabulary. It works on YouTube and on almost every website with video or audio; if a video has no subtitles, the extension listens to the audio and writes them itself;
  • 📄 on web pages and PDFs (Parole al Volo): hover a word on any page to get its card; select some text to translate it all at once.

Vocabulary, known words, service keys, voices and Anki are shared by both.

Browsers: Edge, Chrome, Brave (and other Chromium-based browsers) and Firefox.

Interface language. The extension's menus are in Italian. Tick Interfaccia in questa lingua (interface in this language) under Traduci in (translate into) in the popup and the whole interface — popup, cards, pages — is shown in the language you translate into (translated once with Google Translate and remembered). In this guide buttons are written as they appear in Italian, in bold, followed by their meaning.

1. What it does

Feature What you get
🎬 Two lines Original on top, translation below (or the other way round), right on the video.
🧠 Translation that follows the conversation With Gemini and the other AI models, each line is translated with the surrounding lines in mind.
👆 Interactive words Hover a word to see its meaning; ✨ Spiega con… (explain with…) gives the full explanation in that sentence.
🔗 Translation ↔ original Hover a translated word and the original word it comes from lights up. Articles and prepositions that only the translation needs are marked.
🗣️ Pronunciation Hear any word; the video volume drops automatically while it speaks.
⭐ Vocabulary Save words and expressions with their sentence and the moment in the video; export everything to Anki.
🎙️ Audio transcription Video without subtitles? The audio is transcribed with Whisper (free with a Groq key).
📄 Full text A page with the whole text of the video, to read, copy or download (.txt, .srt).
✏️ Corrections Redo or fix a badly translated or transcribed line with one click.
💸 Low usage Everything is saved: watching a video again costs nothing. If a free service runs out of requests, another one takes over automatically.
📄 Web pages On any page: word card on hover, translation of the text you select.
📕 PDF A PDF reader of its own with the same cards (scanned books too, when they contain recognised text).

2. Installing and updating

Edge, Chrome, Brave

  1. Unzip sottotitoli-parole-chrome-vX.Y.Z.zip into a folder you won't delete (for example Documents\Sottotitoli Doppi).
  2. Open edge://extensions (in Chrome: chrome://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder from step 1.
  5. Pin the extension icon to the toolbar (puzzle icon → pin) so the popup is one click away.

Firefox

  1. Unsigned extensions only install in Firefox Nightly or Developer Edition: in about:config set xpinstall.signatures.required to false.
  2. Open about:addons, click the gear ⚙ → Install Add-on From File… and choose sottotitoli-parole-firefox-vX.Y.Z.xpi.
  3. In about:addons → the extension → Permissions, "Access your data for all websites" must be on. If Concedi accesso (grant access) appears in the popup, click it.

Updating without losing anything

  • Edge/Chrome: unzip the new version into the same folder, replacing the old files, then click ↻ on the extension's card in edge://extensions and reload open tabs.
  • Firefox: install the new .xpi over the old one.

⚠️ Never remove the extension to update it: removing it also deletes your keys, saved translations and vocabulary.

Videos and audio files on your computer

For files opened in the browser (addresses starting with file:///): in edge://extensions open the extension's Details and turn on Allow access to file URLs.

If you used the two separate extensions

Sottotitoli Doppi and Parole al Volo are no longer released separately: this one replaces both. It starts empty: 1. install it next to the old ones (don't remove them yet); 2. bring over your known words and vocabulary: here press ⇄ Sincronizza adesso on the Parole che conosco page and ⇄ Sincronizza con le altre estensioni in the vocabulary (needs Sottotitoli Doppi 5.20.1 / Parole al Volo 1.13.1 or later), or use Esporta in un file in the old one and Importa da un file… here (for the vocabulary: 📒 Vocabolario → ⇄ Esporta in the old one and ⇄ Importa… here); 3. keys and settings come along with ⇄ (only those you haven't set here yet); 4. for the yt-dlp helper: open its page from the popup (🎙 Trascrizione → ⚙ Programma per yt-dlp) and run the installer again, so it recognises this extension too; 5. when everything works, remove the old two: with both active, cards appear twice.

3. Getting started (5 minutes)

  1. Open the popup (click the extension icon) and at the top choose Traduci in (translate into): your language.
  2. With no keys Google Translate is used: you can start straight away.
  3. Open a YouTube video in the language you're learning and turn on subtitles (CC). The two lines appear.
  4. Hover a word in the original line: the video pauses and a card shows its meaning.

For much better translations (recommended, free): 1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey with your Google account and click Create API key (no credit card needed). 2. In the popup, 🔑 Servizi → Gemini (services): paste the key and press Enter. The model is chosen automatically; with the first key, subtitles switch to Gemini (change it in 📊 Consumo e modelli → Modello per ogni compito).

For videos without subtitles you also need a free Groq key: see section 11.

4. The popup

The extension button opens a single popup, for videos and for pages alike. At the top:

  • 📜 Testo del video (the video's text: every line of the open video, with its translation — section 12), 📒 Vocabolario (vocabulary) and ✓ Parole che conosco (words I know);
  • the two master switches: Sottotitoli doppi nei video (dual subtitles in videos) and Parole al Volo sulle pagine e nei PDF (Parole al Volo on pages and in PDFs, Alt+Shift+Y);
  • Traduci in (translate into): your language, for everything (every Google Translate language, about 130, also shown by its own name). Below it, Interfaccia in questa lingua (interface in this language): when ticked, all the extension's text (popup, cards, vocabulary, transcription and PDF reader pages…) is shown in that language. The first time takes a couple of seconds (the text is translated with Google, free), then it's remembered;
  • Consumo delle richieste (request usage): how much the extension uses the services with a key on its own, from best quality to lowest usage (🏆 Qualità, ⚖️ Bilanciato, 💰 Risparmio, 🆓 Solo gratis: section 14);
  • when you're watching a live stream, the «🔴 Questo video è una diretta» (this video is a live stream) box with the choice between normal and synced subtitles.

Then the sections, each opening with a click:

Section What's inside
🔑 Servizi (services) Only the services' keys (with Mostra, Copia, Crea una chiave — create a key), each service's default model, the address of Ollama/LM Studio and of the Whisper server on your computer (section 13).
📊 Consumo e modelli (usage and models) Who does each job and the more technical choices: today's requests per service, backup services, Modello per ogni compito (model for each task: for every task, subtitles and transcription included, a service menu and a model menu; service and exact model), Ordine dei servizi (service order), daily request limit, maximum lead of synced live streams (sections 13 and 14).
🎙 Trascrizione (transcription) Videos without subtitles: transcribe this video, redo the transcription, the mode, the spoken language, synced live streams, the yt-dlp helper (section 11).
📄 Pagine e PDF (pages and PDFs) Card on hover, translation of selected text, the text language, the PDF reader (sections 8 and 9).
📖 Scheda delle parole (word card) What the word card shows, in videos and on pages: IPA pronunciation, etymology, frequency, examples, tables, dictionaries… (section 7).
🎨 Aspetto dei sottotitoli (subtitle appearance) What to show, sizes, colours, position, Latin letters above non-Latin alphabets.
🗒 Aspetto delle schede (card appearance) Text size, width, colours and position of the word cards (videos and pages).
🔈 Voce (voice) The pronunciation voice for each language.
🗃 Anki Decks, automatic sending, pronunciation and images on cards, 🔄 Aggiorna le carte già in Anki (update the cards already in Anki).
⚙ Altro (more) Hide the site's own subtitles, turn the extension off on a site, 📋 Copia il registro per segnalare un problema (copy the log to report a problem), keyboard shortcuts.

Everything is shared between videos and pages: keys, language, vocabulary, known words, voices, Anki, the yt-dlp helper. These have a setting of their own instead: the service for pages (under 📊 Consumo e modelli → Modello per ogni compito → Testi delle pagine e PDF, page and PDF text), disabled sites, known words coloured on pages and the Latin letters in the page card.

5. Where it works

  • YouTube, including YouTube videos embedded in other websites, and YouTube Music (music.youtube.com): the DS button and the dual subtitles appear there too.
  • Any website with video or audio: Vimeo, VK, news sites, podcasts, online courses, live TV sites. If the video has its own subtitles they are used and translated; otherwise you can have them transcribed.
  • Media files opened directly in the browser, from a link or from your computer (mp4, webm, mp3, m4a, ogg, wav, flac…): transcription starts by itself when you press Play.
  • It does not work on DRM-protected sites (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video…): the browser doesn't let extensions listen to their audio. The popup's status line tells you.
  • To turn the extension off on a particular site: popup → ⚙ Altro → Attiva su questo sito (more → enable on this site) for videos, 📄 Pagine e PDF → Attiva su questo sito for pages.

6. Subtitles on the video

Turning them on and off: the DS button

  • On YouTube there's a DS button in the player's control bar, next to CC. On other sites it's a small "DS" in the top-right corner of the video.
  • On (yellow border): dual subtitles are shown. Off: they disappear and transcription stops, so no quota is used.
  • If the dual subtitles disappear for any reason, press DS and they come back. Repeated or double clicks on DS count as one (before, they turned it on and off in a loop).
  • Steadier YouTube CC: if CC is on but the text doesn't reach Sottotitoli Doppi, after 6 seconds the extension turns CC off and on by itself (once per video); the subtitle text is downloaded again with three attempts and, when you turn CC back on, it is sent again even if it's the same as before.
  • The same switch is in the popup: Sottotitoli doppi nei video (dual subtitles in videos).

The two buttons next to the subtitles

Hover the subtitles and two buttons appear on the right: - ⚑ — Wrong line? Opens a card with: - ↻ Ritraduci (translate again): asks for that line's translation again (it jumps the queue); - 🎙 Ritrascrivi (transcribe again, only for transcribed lines): Whisper listens to that line again with its most accurate model; - ✎ Correggi a mano (correct by hand): type the right original and/or translation yourself; - ✕ Togli (remove): for invented lines, e.g. during music.

Corrections are saved with the video and are included in downloaded files. - ⧉ — Copy or look up the line. Pauses the video and shows the line, which you can select with the mouse: - Copia originale, Copia traduzione, Copia tutte e due (copy original / translation / both): if you select part of the text, only that part is copied; - Apri in: Google Traduttore · DeepL · Reverso (open in): the line (or the selected part) opens in a new tab with the languages already set. Reverso shows words inside many real sentences. For language pairs Reverso can't open directly (e.g. Czech), the text is copied: just paste it into Reverso's box.

Appearance

Popup → 🎨 Aspetto dei sottotitoli (subtitle appearance): what to show (both lines, original only, translation only), translation above or below, text size and translation size, colours, background, height on the video, font. Changes show immediately; Ripristina l'aspetto predefinito restores the defaults.

Popup → ⚙ Altro → Nascondi i sottotitoli del sito: hides the site's own subtitles so you don't see them twice.

7. Learning with words

The word card

  • Hover a word in the original line: the video pauses and a card shows its meaning from Google's dictionary (free, no quota used).
  • Click the word: the card stays open, with Google's meaning (no tokens used).
  • ✨ Spiega con … (explain with…, the button in the card): asks your chosen service (Gemini, Groq, Mistral, Claude…) for its meaning in that sentence: base form (e.g. went → go), part of speech, explanation and an example, plus pronunciation and etymology if missing. One request, only when you press the button: the model never starts by itself.
  • On the card:
  • 🔈 pronunciation with the synthetic voice (the code next to it, e.g. DA, shows the language); when a real recording of the word exists (Wiktionary), 🔊 appears next to it. Everywhere: 🔊 = real recording, 🔈 = synthetic voice;
  • ☆ save to your vocabulary;
  • ↺ Riascolta la frase (replay the line): goes back to the start of the line and stops at the end;
  • Copia la parola / Copia la frase (copy word / line);
  • Apri in: Google Translate, DeepL, Reverso, WordReference (between English and Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese) and Wiktionary (explains almost any word in almost any language).
  • Close the card with × or Esc. When you move the mouse away from the subtitles the video resumes (you can turn this off: 📖 Scheda delle parole → Pausa del video quando passi sulle parole, pause the video when hovering words).
  • Explanations are saved even after closing the browser: the same word in the same sentence is never requested twice.

Learning tools

Google dictionary (same request as the translation, free): translations ranked by usage with reverse translations on hover, definitions in the word's own language, synonyms and "see also", and "Did you mean…" in the spelling check. Definitions also come from the Wiktionary edition of the word's language (or English Wiktionary), each with translate. Aa next to the subtitles shows Latin letters above each letter of Cyrillic, Greek, Armenian, Georgian, Hebrew, Japanese kana and Korean (above each word for Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hindi): built-in tables, no service; kanji/Chinese lines get a whole-line reading from Google (free). Anki images can be checked and changed before saving (‹ ›, «altre…», ✕) and in the vocabulary (🖼); sources in popup → Anki. Parole che conosco (popup) opens the frequency list with import/export and ⇄ Sincronizza adesso (direct sync of known words with Parole al Volo, no file); ⇄ in the vocabulary page moves vocabulary and known words to and from Parole al Volo. Level badges are clickable: «livello ≈ B1 ›» (indicative A1–C2 from the frequency rank) opens the words of that level, and for Japanese «JLPT N5 ›» opens the whole JLPT level word list (Jisho). Kanji / Chinese characters one by one: in the card of a Japanese or Chinese word, «Kanji uno per uno» has a button per character; it shows that character alone (meaning, readings in every language — on/kun, pinyin, Cantonese, Korean, Vietnamese —, clickable parts and radical, simplified/traditional form, strokes, JLPT/grade, HSK, Korean school hanja) with «scheda del carattere ›» to open it as a word (vocabulary, Anki) and «‹ Torna alla parola» to go back. Free (built-in Unihan/KANJIDIC/HSK data + Google), no model requests; «☆ salva il kanji» / «☆ salva tutti i kanji» save the single characters to the vocabulary. Only when you press it, ✨ Spiega il kanji con … asks the explanations model (one request) for meanings, readings, common words, parts, a memory hint and the origin; the explanation is saved. In the kanji page the clicked character's card appears next to the list (on the right; at the bottom on narrow screens), even far down; ✕ closes it. On the tiles, on hover, ✓ (known) and ☆ (save to vocabulary) work without opening the card; in the card ☆, «lo conosco?» and ✨ Spiega. The Mostra menu filters: all, to learn, known, saved. Pronunciation on Anki cards (popup → Anki → «Metti la pronuncia nelle carte di Anki», on by default): the card front gets the word's audio — a native speaker's recording (Wiktionary) when there is one for the exact word, otherwise Google's synthetic voice — from the card's Anki button, automatic sending with ☆ (videos and pages) and the vocabulary's «Manda ad Anki». ↺ left of the subtitles replays the sentence, or (yellow) jumps back to the sentence you were reading while the video moved on. Synced live streams (popup → 🎙 Trascrizione → «Dirette sincronizzate»): on YouTube live streams the video is kept behind while the yt-dlp helper reads the audio further on, so transcribed subtitles appear on time (needs the yt-dlp helper with ffmpeg); how far behind: section 11. An orange 🔊* means the recording is of another form of the word. ⚑ Wrong translation? under the meaning lets you redo it with Google or the model, or fix it by hand.

Below the meaning, the word card shows extra help, all free and without keys; each one can be switched off in the popup → 📖 Scheda delle parole (word card): word frequency, "la conosco?" (known words turn green in the subtitles and the popup shows what percentage of the video's words you know), noun gender, a spelling check (LanguageTool or the dictionary; when you click the word, the model also gives a 0-100 score in the same request), the word's etymology, a native speaker recording (the 🔊 button at the top of the card) and a conjugation/declension table (Wiktionary), and up to 3 real example sentences (Tatoeba). Clicking the word, the single request to the model also brings false friends and related words. Next to "↺ Riascolta la frase" there are 🐢 slow and 🔁 ×3. Optional (off by default): an image from Wikimedia Commons on your Anki cards.

From the translation back to the original

  • Hover a word in the translation: the original word it comes from lights up and a label shows it. Click → card for the original word.
  • By default Google makes the link (free, approximate: the label says "circa"). Turn on 📖 Scheda delle parole → Modi di dire e collegamento tra le parole anche dal modello (idioms and word linking from the model too) in the popup to have the AI model make it precise (one request every 8 lines, while hovering).
  • Particles: articles, prepositions and auxiliaries that the translation adds but that don't translate any original word are dotted-underlined. Hover one and the "particella" label tells you which part of the original it belongs to.
  • The other way round: hovering an original word highlights its translation (particles more faintly); without the model, Google dictionary translations of the word are used, matched even with a different ending.

Phrases and expressions

  • 🎯 Rifai più precisa (redo more precisely): under a model's explanation, asks the same service again with more reasoning and knowing what was there before (meaning and explanation): for when it was vague or wrong. One request, only when you press it; the new one stays in the 🗂 saved ones.
  • The sentence translation sits at the bottom of the card, small and in italics (as in Parole al Volo); if the model redid it together with the explanation, that one is shown.
  • 📈 usage over time: the chart of how often the word (or idiom, up to 5 words) appears in books from 1800 to today (Google Books Ngram: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Russian, Hebrew, Chinese). It appears by itself at the bottom of the card after a moment (one free request to Google per word, remembered); hover the chart: a line follows the mouse, year and value appear next to the point, and the text below gives the times per million words. «Apri il grafico completo ↗» opens Google Books Ngram. Turn it off in popup → 📖 Scheda delle parole → «Grafico dell'uso nel tempo» (the 📈 button stays). Every entry in the 📒 vocabulary has its own 📈 Uso nel tempo.
  • Idioms: expressions that can't be translated word by word (break the ice, kick the bucket…) are underlined in orange, with no extra requests: from Wiktionary's idiom dictionary (downloaded once per language, searched on your computer; simple inflections like «kicked the bucket» and person slots like «pulling my leg» are recognised), from the translation when a model translates the subtitles (it lists them in the same request), and the ones already seen. Hover one and the whole expression lights up with its meaning. In a word's card, 🔎 Modi di dire con questa parola (idioms with this word) asks the model (one request). Asking the model on its own — Modi di dire e collegamento tra le parole anche dal modello (videos, one request every 8 lines on hover) and Modi di dire anche dal modello, da soli (pages) — is off by default and on only in «🏆 Qualità»; Modi di dire sottolineati nei sottotitoli (gratis) and Modi di dire sottolineati sulle pagine (gratis) turn the underlining off.
  • Select in the original: press on the first word and drag to the last → card with the meaning of the whole expression.
  • Select in the translation: drag across several translated words → the matching part of the original lights up; the label offers Spiega (explain), Copia (copy) and the lookup links. Close with Esc.

Pronunciation

Voice per language: ⚙ next to 🔈 in the word card lets you pick the voice for that language (saved). Azure Speech (free key, 500,000 characters a month): Microsoft's "Natural" voices in any browser, Firefox included — popup → 🔈 Voce.

The language is taken from the word or the video. The voice is your computer's (Edge's "Natural" voices are excellent); if missing, Google's online voice is used. While it speaks, the video is turned down and then restored.

Popup → 🔈 Voce (voice): language (searchable list of 98 languages), voice, Prova la voce (test the voice).

8. On web pages

On any page (articles, Wikipedia, online books…) words in another language have their card. Over video subtitles these cards don't appear: the ones from section 7 are already there.

  • Hover a word (stay still a moment): the card shows the word, its translation and Google's dictionary, IPA pronunciation and Latin-letter transcription for other alphabets, etymology from Wiktionary (following the chain back to the root, even through other languages; for inflected forms taken from the parent word when the link is certain), the sentence with its translation, 🔈/🔊 to listen, ☆ to save, copy buttons and links to Google Translate, DeepL, Reverso, WordReference and Wiktionary, plus the same learning tools as in videos.
  • With a Gemini or Groq key, "Spiega con Gemini" (explain with…, one request) gives the meaning in that sentence, the base form, whether it's part of an idiom, an example, and in the same request etymology, IPA, false friends (only real ones, compared with words of your language), related words and a spelling score. Everything is written in the language you translate into: an answer in the wrong language is asked again or dropped.
  • Idioms: the card reports the idiom the word belongs to (the other words get a light dashed underline): from Wiktionary's idiom dictionary (free, with a link to its page; the meaning comes with «Spiega con…»), or found earlier. 🔎 Modi di dire con questa parola asks the model for the idioms of the sentence containing that word (one request). They are remembered, and ☆ Salva il modo di dire (save the idiom) puts it in your vocabulary.
  • Selecting several words opens a panel with the translation of the whole text, transcription, IPA on request, listening, ☆ and Anki; with a model also "Traduci meglio" (translate better).
  • 🔎 Modi di dire con questa parola (idioms with this word): finds the idioms that contain the word in general, also outside this sentence: first the free Wiktionary list, then the model (one request, only when you press it) with meaning and example for each. Those in the sentence stay underlined. Every word of a found idiom (and of an expression whose card you open) is a button: press it to look for other idioms with that word. ☆ saves an idiom in the vocabulary. If the model is busy (temporary error, «high demand») the request repeats by itself a couple of times and then moves to the next model.
  • ⚑ Traduzione sbagliata? (wrong translation?): redo with Google or with the model, correct by hand (saved), or change the detected language (also for the whole site).
  • Popup → 📄 Pagine e PDF (pages and PDFs):
  • Parole al Volo sulle pagine e nei PDF, at the top of the popup, turns everything on and off (Alt+Shift+Y);
  • Scheda passando il mouse (card on hover, Alt+Shift+M) and Traduzione del testo selezionato (translation of selected text, Alt+Shift+S) work separately; shortcuts can be changed at edge://extensions/shortcuts (Chrome: chrome://extensions/shortcuts; Firefox: about:addons → ⚙ → Manage Extension Shortcuts);
  • Attiva su questo sito (enable on this site), when the card appears and after how long, Traduzione della frase nella scheda (sentence translation in the card) and Pronuncia la parola quando si apre la scheda (speak the word when the card opens);
  • Lingua del testo (text language): usually detected automatically; if it's often wrong, set it here for all sites or just the open one;
  • known words in green on the page, vocabulary words underlined, idioms found and underlined, Latin letters in the card;
  • 📄 Apri questo PDF qui, 📄 Lettore PDF and Apri sempre i PDF nel lettore dell'estensione (section 9).
  • The service for pages is chosen under 📊 Consumo e modelli → Modello per ogni compito → Testi delle pagine e PDF. IPA, etymology and the other card tools are in 📖 Scheda delle parole, shared with videos.
  • Requests: Google is free; the model only when you press its buttons.

9. PDF files

The browser's PDF viewer doesn't let extensions in, so the extension has its own reader (Mozilla's pdf.js) with the same cards and selection translation.

  • Popup → 📄 Pagine e PDF → «📄 Apri questo PDF qui» (open this PDF here) when the tab shows a PDF, or «📄 Lettore PDF» → «📂 Apri un PDF…» for a file on your computer; «Apri sempre i PDF nel lettore dell'estensione» (always open PDFs in the extension's reader) opens every PDF there automatically.
  • Wrong characters («Pøíliš», «P¯ÌliöÌ», «Ïðèâåò» instead of «Příliš», «Привет»): many older PDFs store text in one encoding but declare another. The reader notices and fixes it by itself (Central European, Cyrillic, Greek, Turkish, Baltic, Hebrew, Arabic, Thai, Vietnamese, Mac and DOS encodings, double-encoded UTF-8, Chinese/Japanese/Korean); the bar says which encoding it fixed, and «Caratteri» lets you choose by hand. Accents written separately (LaTeX: «ˇcesk´y») are joined to their letter.
  • Scanned books (archive.org, Google Books…): the pages are images (JBIG2, JPEG 2000) and are shown; when the PDF contains the recognised text (OCR), cards work too.

10. Your vocabulary

  • On a word or expression card press ☆: it's saved with the sentence, its translation and the moment in the video.
  • Saved words are underlined in light blue in the subtitles of every video.
  • Popup → 📒 Vocabolario (vocabulary): search, reopen the video at the right moment, mark a word as learned (it's no longer highlighted) or delete it.
  • Card layout (one, for direct sending, the file and the update): front: the word in bold with its pronunciation, the sentence in italics with the word in bold, and the image, each on its own line; back: meaning in bold, base form and part of speech, /IPA/, small gender and etymology, the explanation (and the other saved explanations), idioms, the sentence translation in italics, and the link to the video or page. Pronunciation and image are files put in Anki (no longer glued to the end of the front).
  • Rimanda ad Anki (send to Anki again; the entry's button, or Anki in the card): if the card is already in Anki (same language and word) it updates it instead of duplicating; «Manda ad Anki» (all) leaves existing ones as they are and says how many. An error on one card doesn't stop the others: at the end you get the list of failed ones with the reason.
  • 🔄 Aggiorna le carte già in Anki (popup → Anki, or the panel of the same name in the vocabulary): reads the cards tagged «sottotitoli-doppi» or «parole-al-volo» and redoes them: tidy layout, pronunciation (also where there is one already, if you want), missing data (meaning, base form, IPA, gender, etymology, sentence translation and idioms from the dictionary: free) and, only if you tick it, a new explanation, different from the ones already there (added below the others) and idioms with the word looked up by the model (one request each). Choose which cards (all, the language chosen above, only without pronunciation); Controlla (check) says what's missing without changing anything, Ferma (stop) interrupts and ↩ Annulla l'ultimo aggiornamento (undo the last update) puts front and back back as they were. Cards that aren't in the vocabulary are read from the card itself (anything it doesn't recognise stays in the explanation).
  • ✨ Completa (complete, on every vocabulary entry): adds the missing data (free); ✨ + modello also a new explanation and idioms. Then «Rimanda ad Anki» updates the card.
  • Esporta per Anki (export for Anki): downloads a text file. In Anki: File → Import → choose the file (note type "Basic"). Front: the word with its sentence; back: meaning, translation and link to the video.

11. Videos without subtitles: transcribing the audio

If a video has no subtitles, the extension listens to the audio and transcribes it with Whisper; the lines are then translated like any others, with interactive words.

One-time setup

  1. Create a free key at console.groq.com/keys (no credit card).
  2. Popup → 🔑 Servizi → Groq: paste it and press Enter.

Three ways to transcribe (popup → 🎙 Trascrizione)

  • Mentre guardo (while watching): transcribes what you're watching, a few seconds behind. A quick Google translation appears first (slightly transparent) and is then replaced by your chosen service's translation.
  • Completa (complete): as soon as you open the video, the whole audio is downloaded and transcribed in chunks, starting from where you are. Subtitles stay in sync even when you skip around. On YouTube, Vimeo and similar sites it needs the yt-dlp helper (below); direct media files don't.
  • Spenta (off): doesn't start by itself; start it with 🎙 Trascrivi l'audio di questo video (transcribe this video's audio).

Normal videos, transcription starting midway: when the transcription starts (by itself, from the button or after «Rifai»), the video goes back 20 seconds (choose in popup → 🎙 Trascrizione → «Video normali: quando parte la trascrizione, torna indietro di…»: 0, 10, 20, 30, 60 s), as in synced live streams: the lines are ready when you get there. Once per video, not in the first seconds, and not if the transcript already covers that point.

Redoing the transcription: if it came out badly, 🎙 Rifai la trascrizione (redo the transcription, in 🎙 Trascrizione or in the ⚑ card on the video) deletes it and starts again from scratch; with Groq there's also Rifalla con il modello più preciso (redo it with the most accurate model: whisper-large-v3, slower but makes fewer mistakes).

You can choose a mode for all videos without subtitles and a different one for this video. «Completa» only for videos up to… minutes prevents a long film opened by mistake from using up your quota.

The yt-dlp helper

Browsers don't let extensions download audio from YouTube, so a small helper program is installed once. It's needed for Completa transcription on YouTube, Vimeo and similar sites and for synced live streams. 1. Popup → 🎙 Trascrizione → ⚙ Programma per yt-dlp: installa o controlla (yt-dlp helper: install or check). 2. Download the installer and run it (on Windows double-click; if Windows warns you: More info → Run anyway). 3. Go back to the page and press the check button: it must say it is installed and working.

yt-dlp updates itself; when the extension needs a newer helper program, the page says so («vecchio: rilancia l'installatore», old: run the installer again). The audio is only used for transcription and then deleted (YouTube's terms don't allow downloading videos).

Transcription language

  • Lingua parlata (spoken language): leave it on automatic detection. The extension decides the language from the first chunks with enough speech and then forces it on Whisper, so it doesn't switch languages or write an English translation instead of a transcription. Real language changes in the video are still followed.
  • If Whisper gets a video's language wrong, choose it yourself (the list has a search box).

Corrected transcription

✎ Correggi la trascrizione (correct the transcription): a model fixes the words Whisper misheard and removes sentences invented during music or noise, before translation (small corrections in the same language only). - solo le frasi incerte (only uncertain sentences, default): Whisper says how sure it is of each sentence; only the uncertain ones are corrected. About half the requests; - tutte le frasi (all sentences); - spenta (off): sentences stay as Whisper heard them.

With Gemini and the other models that translate, the correction happens in the same request as the translation (no extra request). With Google, DeepL or Microsoft a separate model (Gemini or Groq) does it, in batches. DeepL translates very well, but it also translates the misheard words: the correction is still useful. With YouTube subtitles it's never needed (no Whisper).

How far behind the live stream

With synced live streams (popup → 🎙 Trascrizione → Quanto indietro guardi la diretta, how far behind you watch the stream): 20 s (fast), 45 s (balanced), 90 s (saving), 2 minutes (maximum saving).

The further the video is behind the live edge, the more time there is to prepare subtitles before they're needed, and the less you spend: - audio goes to Whisper in longer pieces (about 5–10 s with 20 s; 20–30 s with 2 minutes): fewer Groq requests, and Whisper understands better with more context; - transcribed sentences wait and are translated in groups. A group is sent when there are enough sentences (12 with 20 s, up to 24 with 2 minutes) or when the first waiting sentence is about to appear on the video: 20 seconds before with 20 s, 15 with 45 s, 10 with 90 s and 2 minutes.

Why waiting until 10 seconds before instead of 20 saves requests: the sentences to translate are always the same (each one is translated once). Only how many requests they are split into changes. Example: sentence A is ready 35 seconds before it appears. Translating 20 seconds before means waiting 15 seconds and sending A with the sentences that arrived in those 15 seconds (about 3). Translating 10 seconds before means waiting 25 seconds (about 5 sentences). Same sentences, fewer requests; tokens are nearly the same (slightly fewer: the model's instructions are repeated less often). Free quotas mostly count requests. If the good translation arrives late, Google's quick one is shown meanwhile.

In exchange, the further behind you are, the later you see the stream (the chat runs ahead of you). When paused nothing is transcribed beyond the maximum lead (90 s by default, in 📊 Consumo e modelli).

Transcribing on your own computer

With a Whisper server compatible with the OpenAI API (e.g. Speaches): its address (usually http://localhost:8000/v1) in 🔑 Servizi → Server Whisper sul tuo computer, then in 📊 Consumo e modelli → Modello per ogni compito → Trascrizione dell'audio choose «Server Whisper sul tuo computer» and the model (↻ loads the server's list).

Firefox

To capture audio Firefox has to mute the video; the extension immediately sends the sound back to your speakers. If you hear nothing or transcription doesn't start, click the video once.

12. The "Trascrizione e traduzione" page

Open it from the popup with 📜 Testo del video (the video's text). It contains the whole text of the current video.

  • Testo unico (single text): original and translation in paragraphs (or just one of them), for reading.
  • Frase per frase (e correzioni) (line by line, with corrections): every line with its time and the buttons ⧉ (copy), ↻ (translate again), 🎙 (transcribe again), ✎ (correct), ✕ (remove).
  • Select and copy: select part of the text with the mouse → a bar appears above it with Copia (copy) and links to Google Translate, DeepL, Reverso (if you select in the translation, the direction is reversed). The page never redraws while you're selecting.
  • Copia il testo (copy the text), Scarica testo (.txt), Sottotitoli originali / tradotti / doppi (.srt): to keep them or use them in another player.
  • Trascrivere un file intero (transcribe a whole file): from your computer or from the tab's video, without watching it. Works even with no video open.
  • Carica sottotitoli da file (.srt, .vtt): for films that come with separate subtitle files.
  • 🔁 Rifai solo la traduzione (redo only the translation, popup → 🎙 Trascrizione): deletes only the translation (of a transcript or of YouTube subtitles) and redoes it with the chosen service, leaving the text alone. If the transcription runs ahead and the translation stays behind (slow model, queued requests), after 5 seconds the lines already past get Google's quick translation and the queue restarts.
  • Rifai la traduzione / Rifai la trascrizione (redo the translation / transcription): deletes what's saved for the video and does it again (your manual corrections are kept).

Translating with a chat, without using any quota

  1. Press Copia trascrizione (copy transcription): it copies the numbered lines with instructions (long videos in parts of 250 lines: the button tells you which part it will copy).
  2. Paste them into a chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) and copy its answer.
  3. Press Incolla traduzione (paste translation), paste the answer and confirm. The extension reads the numbered lines, checks that it really is a translation and uses it on the video. It stays saved.

Keys are pasted in the popup, 🔑 Servizi (services): each service has its own box (with Crea una chiave, create a key, to reach the right page) and, for language models, the Modello predefinito (default model) menu (↻ Carica modelli, load models, fetches the real list from the service; «✎ un altro» to type a name). Who does each job (subtitles, pages, transcription, explanations…) is chosen in 📊 Consumo e modelli → Modello per ogni compito.

Service Cost Quality What you need

Gemini ⭐ recommended free excellent, follows the conversation key from aistudio.google.com/apikey
Groq free good, very fast key from console.groq.com/keys (the same as for transcription)

Gemini, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter, Claude and local models are AI language models: besides translating, they understand the conversation, explain words in context, find idioms and correct transcriptions. Google, Microsoft and DeepL are classic translators: they translate well but don't do the rest.

Keys can be shown and copied with the Mostra (show) and Copia (copy) buttons next to the box (handy for the Kodi add-on). Don't show them in videos or screenshots: anyone who sees a key can use it.

Backup services

When the chosen service has used up today's free requests, a yellow notice appears in the popup: it says when they come back (Gemini quotas reset at midnight Pacific Time) and which service is used meanwhile. "Riprova … adesso" (retry … now) resumes the video's translation with the chosen service straight away (handy if you changed key or model). With Finite le richieste gratuite, passa a un altro servizio con la chiave (when free requests run out, switch to another service with a key, on by default), when a service uses up its daily quota the extension carries on with the first other service you've entered a key for, in the order set under Ordine dei servizi (by default Gemini → Groq → Mistral → OpenRouter → Ollama/LM Studio → Claude → Microsoft → DeepL), and finally Google. The exhausted service is skipped until the next day. With Gemini, Groq and Mistral only the used-up model is blocked: the other models of the same service are tried first (Model for each task); the yellow notice says so.

Model for each task

In 📊 Consumo e modelli, the Modello per ogni compito sub-menu (opens with a click) has a service menu per job: video subtitles, page and PDF text, audio transcription (Groq Whisper or the server on your computer, with the Whisper model menu), explanations («✨ Spiega con…») (etymologies and kanji explanations too), transcript correction, IPA for selected text and word and idiom linking. «Automatico» uses the first ready model in Ordine dei servizi; if the chosen model doesn't answer or is out of requests for today, the first ready one replaces it.

Under each task there's the list of models of the service actually doing that task now, in order. The first ready one is used (in bold). On Gemini, Groq and Mistral free limits are per model: when a model runs out of today's requests only that model is blocked («finito oggi», used up today) and the next model is used automatically; only when all are used up does the next service in Ordine dei servizi take over. Example: subtitles with Gemini Flash-Lite; Flash-Lite used up → Gemini Flash; both used up → Groq. - ↑ ↓ change the order; Aggiungi (add) is a menu of the service's models (↻ loads the real list; «✎ un altro» to type one): the chosen one goes first; ✕ removes a model you added; - at the bottom, as a backup, there are always the service's default model (in 🔑 Servizi) and the suggested models for that kind of task (for Gemini: Flash-Lite and Flash; lightest first for translating, best first for explaining).

A light model for translating and a better one for explaining split the requests between the two.

Which models for which task (suggestions):

Task What matters Suggested
Video subtitles and live streams many lines, speed, many daily requests gemini-flash-lite-latest; Groq (very fast); a light model on your computer (section 15)
Explanations and etymologies quality, few requests gemini-flash-latest; Mistral (European languages); Claude (paid)
Transcript correction understanding misheard words gemini-flash-lite-latest or Groq
Word and idiom linking speed as for subtitles
IPA for selected text knowing phonetics gemini-flash-latest

The request usage modes (section 14) set Gemini's models by themselves: Flash-Lite for translating and correcting, Flash for explaining (Flash for everything in «Qualità»).

Service order

The Ordine dei servizi (service order) sub-menu lists every service with its state (ready, key missing, model missing, used up for today): - ↑ ↓ change the order; unticking the box removes a service from the queue; - Usa adesso (use now) moves it to the top and selects it right away for translating too, then runs a test; - Prova (test) translates a sample sentence and shows ✓ Funziona (with the translation and the seconds taken) or ✗ Non funziona with the reason (for Ollama: program not running, OLLAMA_ORIGINS missing, no model chosen…).

The order is used for backup services, for ✨ Spiega con… (when translating with Google, DeepL or Microsoft, the first ready model in the queue — only when you press the button), for IPA pronunciation and for transcript correction. To try Ollama: in 🔑 Servizi press «Carica modelli» and pick the model, then here Usa adesso.

Spiegazioni («✨ Spiega con…») (explanations), in Modello per ogni compito: a model used only for explanations, different from the subtitle one. For example subtitles with Gemini (fast) and explanations with Ollama (on your computer: no limits, no requests used up). If the chosen model doesn't answer or is out of requests for today, the first ready model in the queue explains instead, and the card says so; after 5 minutes the chosen one is tried again.

Models that "think" before answering (Gemma 4, Qwen 3…: Ollama shows "Thinking…") answer without the reasoning when used by the extension, much faster. If your computer struggles (fans, external screen dropping out), use a smaller model such as gemma4:e2b.

At the top of the popup, Consumo delle richieste (request usage) chooses how much the extension uses the services with a key on its own. It sets the other menus (live stream, correction, idioms, Gemini models); if you then change one by hand, the menu shows ✎ Personalizzato (custom).

In Solo gratis Google Translate translates (your chosen service stays saved for when you change mode) and transcription of videos without subtitles only starts if you start it. Models work only when you press a button («✨ Spiega con…», «🔎 Modi di dire con questa parola»…).

In 📊 Consumo e modelli: - Richieste di oggi (today's requests) per service (Gemini, Groq Whisper…), counted by the extension. The quota day ends at midnight Pacific Time; - Limite di richieste al giorno (daily request limit) per service: once reached, the backup service takes over as when the free quota runs out (e.g. Gemini 300, to keep some for explanations); - Diretta sincronizzata: trascrivi al massimo così avanti rispetto al video (maximum lead, 60 s – 4 min): beyond that, audio waits; also when paused.

Other ways to save: - Nothing is translated twice: translations, transcriptions, explanations and corrections are all saved. Watching a video again, even one you only partly watched, costs nothing. - Translations are requested in groups of 120 lines: a two-hour film costs about 12 Gemini requests. - Hovering uses Google's free dictionary; the AI model is only used when you click. - Gemini's default model is gemini-flash-lite-latest, the one with the most free daily requests. - Gemini quotas reset at midnight Pacific Time. You can see your usage at aistudio.google.com. - For a whole film without touching any quota: Copia trascrizione / Incolla traduzione with a chat (section 12).

Free, unlimited and private. It's slower than online services, small models make more mistakes with less common languages, etymologies and long explanations, and it answers one request at a time. A good use: subtitles and live streams with the model on your computer, explanations with Gemini (📊 Consumo e modelli → Modello per ogni compito).

17. Privacy

  • Your keys and everything you save (translations, vocabulary, corrections) stay in your browser. There is no server behind the extension.
  • Text is only sent to the services you choose (Google, Gemini, Groq…). On free plans some services (e.g. Gemini) may use the text to improve their models.
  • The yt-dlp helper downloads audio only to transcribe it and deletes it straight away.
